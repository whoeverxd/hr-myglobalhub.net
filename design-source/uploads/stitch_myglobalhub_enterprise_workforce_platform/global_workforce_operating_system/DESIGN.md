## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, modern enterprise SaaS environment tailored for complex workforce management, global payroll compliance, multi-timezone time tracking, and bi-directional Jira Cloud project sync. The visual voice blends the structural authority of top-tier enterprise human capital platforms (Rippling, Workday, BambooHR) with the agility and responsiveness of modern developer tools.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Sovereign Trust & Precision:** Users manage critical payroll runs, cross-border tax compliance, and auditable engineering time allocations. Visual clarity and absolute legibility take precedence over ornamental distractions.
- **Global Interconnectivity:** The aesthetic embodies worldly reach through deep oceanic navy foundations, vibrant emerald growth signals, and rich amber metrics that indicate operational momentum.
- **Frictionless Enterprise Velocity:** Dense data tables, nested approval drawers, and real-time synchronizations feel lightweight, clean, and organized through crisp spatial rhythm and refined containers.

### Design Style: Modern Enterprise Architectural
The system applies a disciplined **Corporate Modern** style with subtle atmospheric cues:
- Monolithic midnight navy shell containers (`#0B192C` to `#0A2540`) for primary navigation and contextual global tools.
- Multi-tier neutral surfaces with pristine white canvas layers (`#FFFFFF`) against cool soft backing tones (`#F8FAFC`).
- High-efficiency hairline boundaries (`#E2E8F0`) reinforced by ultra-soft, warm-tinted modern shadows that grant organic separation without visual heaviness.
- Micro-hexagonal geometries and dynamic status badging reflecting global coordination nodes.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for high information density, rapid scanning of payroll and sprint status states, and uncompromising WCAG AAA/AA compliance across dense enterprise screens.

### Palette Architecture

- **Primary (`#0A2540` / `#0B192C`):** Deep oceanic navy anchoring navigation panels, main header elements, heavy typography, and primary button interactions. It communicates stability, financial rigor, and executive oversight.
- **Secondary (`#00875A`):** Vivid emerald green reserved for positive monetary balances, validated timesheet runs, successful Jira synchro status, active employee indicators, and primary affirmative actions.
- **Tertiary (`#EAA023`):** Radiant honey amber used for pending actions, payroll cycle cut-offs, Jira blocked tasks, attention-demanding telemetry, and warning micro-badges.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` to `#F8FAFC`):** 
  - Text Primary: `#0F172A` for high-contrast tabular figures and headlines.
  - Text Secondary: `#475569` for meta-labels, sub-headers, and column descriptors.
  - Borders & Dividers: `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1` for clean structural cell definition.
  - Canvas & Backgrounds: `#F8FAFC` for page canvases and `#FFFFFF` for primary card and data grid modules.

### Semantic Status Tokens
- **Danger / Critical:** `#DE350B` (payroll discrepancies, failed API handshakes, overdue approvals).
- **Info / Synchronization:** `#0052CC` (Jira sync queues, active work session notices).
- **Muted Surface / Fill:** `#F1F5F9` (hover fills, tag backings, inactive table column states).

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the unified typographic engine across all device contexts. It provides a geometric warmth combined with crisp modern ascenders and open apertures, maintaining immaculate readability in ultra-dense analytical sheets, Jira issue links, and financial rosters.

### Type Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Headlines & Metric Totals:** Rendered with weights `700` and `800` using tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor dashboards and module headers with executive weight.
- **Enterprise Body:** Sized purposefully at `14px` (`body-md`) and `13px` (`body-sm`) with a 1.4-1.5 proportional line height. This balances maximum row visibility with zero eyestrain during prolonged operational cycles.
- **Data & Numeric Formats (`tabular-num`):** All financial figures, hours logged, payroll deductions, and timestamps must leverage the CSS property `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`. This prevents alignment flutter when values update dynamically in high-frequency tables.
- **Caps & Micro Labels (`label-sm`):** Rendered at `11px` in bold uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.04em`) for table column descriptors, tag indicators, and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture implements a high-efficiency **fixed-fluid hybrid model** designed to optimize workstation viewports (1440px to 1920px+) while gracefully degrading to tablet and field mobile environments.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Persistent App Shell:** 
  - Left Global Rail / Sidebar: Fixed `260px` (expandable) or `72px` (collapsed icon-only mode) bathed in `#0B192C`.
  - Top Global Utility Bar: Fixed `64px` height, containing context switchers, search (`/`), sync health, and profile controls.
  - Main Workbench Canvas: Fluid grid spanning 12 columns with standard `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Data Density Presets:**
  - **Dense (Default for Tables & Grids):** Cell vertical padding of `0.5rem` (`space-sm`), compact input heights at `36px`.
  - **Comfortable (Cards & Summary Views):** Card internal padding of `1.25rem` (`space-lg`), standard form inputs at `42px`.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Full 12-column analytical layout, multi-pane drill-down sheets, side-by-side timesheet vs. Jira task comparisons.
- **Tablet / Small Laptop (768px - 1279px):** Sidebar automatically collapses into icon rail mode (`72px`); grid steps to 8 columns with `1rem` gutters; secondary metric panels stack below primary data tables.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Sidebar transitions to an off-canvas drawer; top navigation condenses to title and hamburger trigger; grids collapse to a single column with horizontal swipe-scroll for tabular data views.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined layering scheme: deep flat structural foundations paired with soft, multidimensional ambient shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiering
1. **Foundation Level (Sidebar & Global Canvas):** `#0B192C` and `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, flat structural anchor.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Tables):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill resting on subtle boundaries (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) augmented by a feather-light shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03);`
3. **Surface Tier 2 (Hover States, Active Cards, Dropdowns):** Elevated interactive components incorporate a secondary ambient spread:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04);`
4. **Surface Tier 3 (Modals, Slide-over Drawers, Floating Timers):** Heavy focus overlays paired with deep diffused containment:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06);`

### Border & Division Philosophy
No card relies on heavy outlines or dark separations. Structure is maintained using crisp `1px` lines tinted with slate tones (`#E2E8F0`), allowing high-density data tables and metrics to feel organized without feeling boxy or overwhelming.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is **Rounded (Level 2)**, presenting an approachable, modern executive touch while preserving crisp corporate lines.

### Corner Radius System
- **Micro Radius (`rounded-sm` - 4px):** Checkboxes, status dots, inline Jira task chips, and sub-table row actions.
- **Component Radius (`rounded-md` - 8px):** Primary form inputs, select dropdowns, action buttons, table cell groupings, and toolbar containers.
- **Card & Container Radius (`rounded-lg` - 12px):** KPI metric overview cards, high-level data grid containers, modal dialogue panels, and payroll summary cards.
- **Sheet & Large Surface Radius (`rounded-xl` - 16px):** Slide-over payroll approval sheets and comprehensive profile overlays.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full` - 9999px):** Live attendance badges, synchronization status pills, user avatars, and counter tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep navy background (`#0A2540`), solid white text, `8px` corner radius, `40px` height (`32px` in dense mode), medium weight (`600`). On hover, smoothly transitions to `#16375A` with a micro-lift shadow.
- **Secondary / Success Action:** Emerald green background (`#00875A`), white text, used for actions like "Run Payroll," "Approve Timesheets," or "Sync Jira Sprints."
- **Tertiary / Outline:** White surface with `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover brings `#F8FAFC` background and `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Destructive:** Soft crimson background (`#FEE2E2`), crimson text (`#DE350B`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Status indicators feature a semi-transparent, soft-tinted background (10-15% opacity) accompanied by high-contrast foreground text and an integrated 6px status bead:
  - **Active / Synced:** Background `#E3FCEF`, Text `#00875A`, Dot `#00875A`.
  - **In Review / Pending:** Background `#FEF3C7`, Text `#B45309`, Dot `#EAA023`.
  - **Discrepancy / Blocked:** Background `#FEE2E2`, Text `#DE350B`, Dot `#DE350B`.
  - **Jira Issue Chip:** Background `#EDF2F7`, Text `#0A2540`, leading icon with official issue key format (e.g., `ENG-402`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `8px` border radius, `14px` text size. Focus states display a `2px` outer glow in `#0A2540` at 15% opacity and a primary navy border ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px x 16px` with subtle `#CBD5E1` border. Active state fills with `#0A2540` or `#00875A` showing a crisp white verification checkmark.

### Cards & Data Tables
- **Cards:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders and `12px` corner radius. Card headers feature upper-left bold labelling with quick-action icon clusters on the right.
- **Data Tables:**
  - Column headers set in uppercase `label-sm` (`11px`, `#475569`) with subtle bottom border.
  - Alternating rows avoid heavy zebra striping; instead, clean white rows highlight with `#F8FAFC` on hover.
  - Numeric columns default to right alignment using `tabular-nums`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Active Time Tracker Bar:** Slim anchored widget displaying active Jira task title, real-time counter, project tag, and an emerald green glowing recording beacon.
- **Payroll Breakdown Card:** Dual-split card showcasing total gross, statutory employer deductions, employee withholdings, and net transfer amounts, with amber notification bars for impending cut-off dates.
